NORDIC BAY UNIVERSITY — COURSE REGISTRATION AND ENROLLMENT RULES

1. Standard Semester Credit Load
Full-time students at Nordic Bay University are expected to register for 30 ECTS credits per semester (typically four courses of 7.5 ECTS each). Students with a cumulative GPA of 3.5 or higher may request advisor approval to overload up to 37.5 ECTS credits in a single semester.

2. Registration Windows
Course registration opens via the Student Portal three weeks before the start of each semester:
- Autumn Semester Registration: Opens August 10 at 08:00 and closes September 7 at 23:59.
- Spring Semester Registration: Opens December 20 at 08:00 and closes January 22 at 23:59.
Students must confirm their attendance in the Student Portal within the first 5 business days of the semester, or their course seats will be automatically released to waitlisted students.

3. Add/Drop and Course Withdrawal Policy
- Add/Drop Period: Students may add or drop courses without academic penalty during the first 10 business days of the semester. Dropped courses during this window do not appear on the official transcript.
- Late Withdrawal: Between Week 3 and Week 8 of the semester, students may withdraw from a course with a "W" (Withdrawn) grade recorded on their transcript. After Week 8, course withdrawal is only permitted for documented medical or family emergencies.

4. Prerequisites and Waitlists
Students cannot register for advanced courses unless all listed prerequisite courses have been completed with a passing grade of E or higher. If a course reaches maximum enrollment capacity, students are placed on an automated waitlist in chronological order of registration attempt.
