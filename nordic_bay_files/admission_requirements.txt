NORDIC BAY UNIVERSITY — ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS AND APPLICATION POLICY

1. General Undergraduate Admission Requirements
To be eligible for undergraduate bachelor's programs at Nordic Bay University, applicants must hold a recognized upper secondary school diploma (high school equivalent) with a minimum cumulative grade point average of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale. Applicants must also demonstrate proficiency in Mathematics at Level 2 or higher.

2. English Language Proficiency
All degree programs at Nordic Bay University are taught in English. Applicants whose previous education was not conducted entirely in English must submit one of the following valid test scores (no older than 24 months):
- IELTS Academic: Minimum overall score of 6.5, with no individual band below 6.0.
- TOEFL iBT: Minimum total score of 90, with at least 20 in writing.
- Cambridge C1 Advanced: Minimum overall score of 180.

3. Master's Program Admission Requirements
Applicants for master's programs must hold an accredited bachelor's degree consisting of at least 180 ECTS credits (or equivalent) in a relevant academic discipline. A statement of purpose (maximum 800 words) and two academic letters of recommendation are required with the online application.

4. Application Deadlines and Fees
- Autumn Semester (starts September 1): Application deadline is April 15 for international applicants and June 1 for domestic applicants.
- Spring Semester (starts January 15): Application deadline is October 15 for international applicants and November 15 for domestic applicants.
- Non-refundable application processing fee: 75 EUR for international applicants; free for domestic and EU/EEA citizens.
- Late applications are only considered for programs with remaining seats and incur an additional 40 EUR late review fee.
