NORDIC BAY UNIVERSITY — STUDENT SUPPORT, HEALTH, AND ACCOMMODATIONS

1. Academic Advising and Tutoring
The Academic Success Center (Student Union Building, Room 204) provides free drop-in academic advising Monday through Friday from 09:00 to 16:00. Free peer tutoring in mathematics, programming, and academic writing is available by booking a 45-minute session through the Student Portal at least 24 hours in advance.

2. Disability and Learning Accommodations
Students with documented disabilities, chronic illnesses, dyslexia, or neurodivergent conditions are entitled to tailored study accommodations, such as 25% to 50% extended examination time, separate quiet exam rooms, or note-taking assistance. To activate accommodations for the current semester, students must submit medical or psychological documentation to the Accessibility Coordinator at least 3 weeks before their first scheduled examination.

3. Counseling and Health Services
The University Health & Counseling Clinic (Building A, Ground Floor) offers up to 6 free, confidential mental health counseling sessions per academic year for all enrolled students. Same-day urgent mental health drop-in slots are available weekdays between 13:00 and 15:00. Basic nursing care and vaccinations are provided free of charge with a valid student ID.

4. Financial Aid, Emergency Grants, and Career Services
- Emergency Hardship Fund: Enrolled students facing sudden, unexpected financial emergencies (such as medical bills or housing crises) may apply for a one-time, non-repayable emergency grant of up to 800 EUR. Decisions are issued within 5 business days.
- Career & Internship Office: Offers CV reviews, mock interviews, and employer networking fairs every October and March. International students can also book free workshops on post-graduation work visa rules.
