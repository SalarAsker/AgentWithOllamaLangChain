NORDIC BAY UNIVERSITY — EXAMINATION RULES AND GRADING POLICY

1. Mandatory Exam Registration and Identification
Students must register for written on-campus examinations in the Student Portal at least 10 calendar days before the scheduled exam date. Unregistered students will not be admitted to the exam hall. On the day of the exam, students must present a valid government-issued photo ID or physical Nordic Bay University student card.

2. Arrival Time and Allowed Materials
- Exam doors open 30 minutes prior to the start time. Students arriving more than 30 minutes late will not be permitted to enter the examination hall.
- No student may leave the exam room during the first 45 minutes of the examination.
- Mobile phones, smartwatches, and wireless earphones must be turned off completely and stored inside closed bags at the front of the room. Only non-programmable calculators and items explicitly listed on the course syllabus are allowed on the desk.

3. Grading Scale
Nordic Bay University uses the seven-point ECTS letter grading scale:
- A: Excellent (90–100%)
- B: Very Good (80–89%)
- C: Good (70–79%)
- D: Satisfactory (60–69%)
- E: Sufficient / Minimum Passing Grade (50–59%)
- FX: Fail — minor supplementary work required (45–49%)
- F: Fail — full re-examination required (below 45%)

4. Re-Examinations and Academic Integrity
Students who receive an F or miss an exam due to illness (with a valid medical certificate submitted within 5 business days) are entitled to take the scheduled re-examination held 4 to 6 weeks after the regular exam period. Any form of plagiarism, unauthorized AI generation on closed assessments, or cheating during an exam is reported to the Disciplinary Board and may result in suspension for up to 6 months.
