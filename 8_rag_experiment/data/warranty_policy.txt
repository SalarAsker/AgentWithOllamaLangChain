NORDICTECH STORE — WARRANTY POLICY

Laptops sold by NordicTech Store include a 24-month limited warranty.

Keyboards and computer mice include a 12-month limited warranty.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal use.

Accidental damage, liquid damage, and unauthorized modifications are not covered
by the limited warranty.

Customers must provide proof of purchase when requesting warranty service.

Warranty requests are reviewed by the customer service department.
