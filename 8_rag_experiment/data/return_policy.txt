
NORDICTECH STORE — RETURN POLICY

Customers may return eligible products within
30 days of delivery.

Products must be returned with their original
accessories and proof of purchase.

Damaged products must be reported within
7 days of delivery.

Customers must contact customer service before
returning a damaged product.

Approved refunds are processed within
5 business days after the returned item
has been inspected.

Return shipping is free when a product
arrives damaged or defective.

For other eligible returns, the customer
is responsible for return shipping costs.
